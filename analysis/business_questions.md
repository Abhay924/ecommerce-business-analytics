# Key Business Questions
1. **Revenue & Growth**: What are the monthly revenue growth rates, seasonality peaks, and net profit margins?
2. **Customer Lifecycle & CLV**: Who are our highest-value customers and how does CLV vary by acquisition channel?
3. **Product Performance**: Which products drive 80% of gross margin (Pareto), and which categories have high return rates?
4. **Retention & Churn**: How quickly do monthly customer cohorts decay after their initial purchase?
5. **Marketing Attribution & ROAS**: Which paid campaigns deliver ROAS > 3.0x and the lowest CAC?
