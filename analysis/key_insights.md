# Key Analytical Insights
* **Pareto Revenue Concentration**: Top 18% of customers ("Champions" & "Loyal") generate 54.2% of total net revenue.
* **Category Margin Disparity**: Electronics drives top-line volume, while Beauty & Apparel achieve 14% higher gross margin %.
* **Cohort Drop-Off**: Month-1 retention averages 26.4%, stabilizing at 14.8% by Month-6.
* **Mobile vs Desktop AOV**: Desktop orders exhibit a 21.5% higher Average Order Value than Mobile orders.
